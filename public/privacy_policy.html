Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 24/08/2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Chat-Pat. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our chat application.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. By using Chat-Pat, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not use the Application.

2. Information We Collect

Because Chat-Pat does not require registration, email addresses, or phone numbers, we collect a very limited amount of data.

a. Information You Provide Directly

  • Username: You are required to input a username to participate in chats. This username is displayed publicly within the chat rooms.
  • Chat Content: Any messages, images, or files you send via the WebSocket connection are temporarily processed and stored to facilitate the conversation.

b. Automatically Collected Information

When you use the Application, certain information is automatically collected by our servers (Durable Objects) and infrastructure:

  • IP Address: Used to route traffic and for basic security/rate-limiting.
  • Browser/Device Metadata: Information such as your operating system, browser type, and screen resolution may be logged for debugging and performance optimization.
  • Connection Timestamps: The time you connect and disconnect from the WebSocket.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the Service: To route your messages to the correct chat room using WebSocket and Durable Object technology.
  • Maintain Security: To monitor for spam, abuse, or malicious activity (e.g., rate-limiting to prevent server overload).
  • Improve Performance: To analyze usage patterns to ensure the "Durable Objects" infrastructure remains stable and fast.
  • Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

4. Data Storage and Retention (Durable Objects)

Chat-Pat utilizes Cloudflare Durable Objects for real-time state management.

  • Storage: Chat messages may be stored ephemerally within Durable Objects to synchronize conversations across users. This storage is transient and exists primarily to facilitate the "live" nature of the chat.
  • Retention Policy: We do not permanently archive chat history. Messages are retained for a short period to allow for connection drops and reconnects.
  • Deletion: Once a conversation is inactive or the Durable Object is evicted, the chat data is automatically and permanently deleted.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. However, we may share data in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We use Cloudflare to host our Durable Objects and WebSocket infrastructure. Your data is processed via their network.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

6. Security

We are committed to protecting your data. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures, including:

  • Encryption: WebSocket connections are secured via TLS/SSL.
  • Isolation: Cloudflare Durable Objects provide isolated compute environments to prevent cross-tenant data breaches.
⚠️ Public Username Warning: Because you are not required to log in, your username is public. We strongly advise against using your real name, email address, or any personally identifiable information as your username.

7. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can request we delete your data.
  • Right to Object: You can object to our processing of your data.

How to Exercise These Rights: Because there is no login system, we cannot verify your identity via an email. To exercise your rights, please contact us directly (see Section 10). We will require proof of connection (such as the IP address used during the session) to identify your data.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
